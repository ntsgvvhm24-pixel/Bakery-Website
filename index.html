About Us

The story behind our bread

A family bakery in Madrid

The Golden Crumb opened in 2010 as a small family bakery. Our bakers start work at 4:00 in the morning so that the bread is warm when you arrive.

We believe that good bread needs time, patience and love. That is why our dough rests for many hours before it goes into the oven.

Our values

  • Fresh every day: everything is baked the same morning
  • Local ingredients: flour, eggs and butter from nearby farms
  • Tradition: old recipes from Madrid and from all over Spain
  • No shortcuts: natural sourdough and slow fermentation

Our history

Year Milestone
2010 We open our first shop in Madrid
2014 We start baking natural sourdough bread
2018 We bake our first San Isidro rosquillas
2023 We open our cake workshop for special celebrations
